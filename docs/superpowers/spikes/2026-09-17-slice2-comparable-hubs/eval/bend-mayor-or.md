# Race A: Bend Mayor — City of Bend, Oregon — General Election 2026-11-03

slug: `bend-mayor-or`

## ⚠️ Ground-truth correction (confidence: high, verified via 3 independent sources)

The task brief lists the candidates as **Melanie Kebler** and **Ron (Rondo) Boozell**. Research shows
**Boozell did not qualify for the November ballot** — he failed to collect the required signatures by
the August 25, 2026 filing deadline. The certified, on-ballot race for Mayor (Position 7) is:

- **Melanie Kebler** (incumbent)
- **Bernadette Strome** (challenger, small-business owner)

This is confirmed independently by (1) bendbulletin.com's Aug 26, 2026 filing-deadline story, (2)
bendsource.com's "Candidates Set for Bend Mayoral and City Council Races" story, and (3) the official
Deschutes County 2026 General Election Voters' Pamphlet, which prints statements for only Kebler and
Strome on p. 9-43 — no Boozell entry exists. Boozell has also been reported separately as arrested for
trespassing at a candidate forum in May 2026 and is a perennial, never-elected candidate. All source
research below targets the real matchup (Kebler vs. Strome); no comparable-source appearances by
Boozell were found for 2026 (unsurprising, since he isn't on the ballot).
