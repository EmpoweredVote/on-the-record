## Organizing bodies (hubs) for this jurisdiction

- **Citizens Clean Elections Commission** (azcleanelections.gov) + **Arizona Media Association** — jointly produce Arizona's statewide/legislative and (per this cycle) congressional general-election debate series. Confirmed as organizer of the CD6 debate.
- **League of Women Voters of Southern Arizona** (lwvtucson.org) — local LWV chapter covering Tucson/CD6; feeds into VOTE411.org.
- **VOTE411.org** (League of Women Voters Education Fund) — national LWV voter-guide portal; has a race/candidate-detail page structure for "Arizona U.S. House of Representatives District 6."
- **Arizona Daily Star / Tucson.com** and **Tucson Sentinel** — local newspaper "Midterm Election Guide 2026" hubs with ongoing CD6 coverage.
- **Tucson Metro Chamber of Commerce** (tucsonchamber.org) — runs a "Candidate Questionnaire," but its published one is titled for AZ **state** Senate/House, not U.S. House.
- **AZPM (Arizona Public Media, PBS/NPR Tucson)** and **KJZZ (NPR Phoenix)** — public broadcasters carrying debate coverage, fact-checks, and reporting with direct candidate quotes.

## Richness summary

The AZ-06 landscape is **thin-to-moderate**: there is one strong, verified Tier-1 source (the Clean Elections/Arizona Media Association debate), but it only covers 2 of the 3 named candidates and Peters (Libertarian) is absent from every source found. Tier 2 (full interviews) is **empty** — no dedicated one-on-one interview with either major candidate was located, only news profiles and debate fact-checks. Tier 3 is present but weak: Ballotpedia's Candidate Connection page-mechanism exists for all three candidates but is **unfilled by all of them**, VOTE411 has an indexed page whose content could not be verified (access blocked), and the two most likely written-questionnaire hosts (AZ Clean Elections' own guide, Tucson Metro Chamber) are confirmed or strongly suspected to be out of scope for a federal race this cycle. Net: usable ground truth is essentially the single Tier-1 debate; everything else is a hub-identified-but-unfilled/unverified gap, and Peters has no comparable source at any tier.
