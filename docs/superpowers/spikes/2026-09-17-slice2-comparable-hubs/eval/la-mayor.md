# Race B: Los Angeles Mayor (2026-11-03)

Candidates in the general (top-two) election: Karen Bass (incumbent) and Nithya Raman. (14 candidates ran in the June 2, 2026 nonpartisan primary, including Spencer Pratt (R), who did not advance; out of scope per the prompt's named two.)

## Organizing bodies (hubs) for this jurisdiction

- **League of Women Voters of Greater Los Angeles** (my.lwv.org/california/greater-los-angeles) and **League of Women Voters of Los Angeles County** (lwvlacounty.org) — local LWV chapters; point to **VOTE411.org** and **Voter's Edge California** (LWV CA / MapLight) as their voter-guide tools.
- **LAist** (KPCC/LAist Studios, NPR/public-radio member station) — runs its own "Voter Guides" hub, its own written candidate questionnaire, and "AirTalk" long-form candidate interviews; also moderates/co-hosts debates and forums. Functions as the de facto dominant neutral hub for this race.
- Neighborhood councils and local chambers (e.g., **Sherman Oaks Neighborhood Council** and **Sherman Oaks/Encino Chamber of Commerce**) — host individual debates; genuinely local/neutral but ad hoc, not a standing jurisdiction-wide body.
- **Los Angeles City Ethics Commission** (clerk.lacity.gov / ethics.lacity.gov) — campaign finance filings only, not a debate/questionnaire host.
- Issue-advocacy coalitions (LA League of Conservation Voters, Sierra Club, SCOPE-LA, etc.) — host single-issue forums; explicitly **not neutral** (advocacy), noted below.

## Richness summary

The LA Mayor landscape is **rich**, driven almost entirely by one outlet, LAist/KPCC, which supplies a neutral source at every tier: a Tier-1 debate (co-hosted/broadcast), Tier-2 matched long-form AirTalk interviews with both candidates from the same interviewer and venue, and a Tier-3 written questionnaire with both candidates answering the same questions in their own words — plus both candidates independently completing Ballotpedia's Candidate Connection survey. The one confirmed gap is the LWV/VOTE411 track: unlike AZ-06, no specific VOTE411 or Voter's Edge page for this race was locatable, even though the organizing bodies (LWV Greater LA, LWV LA County) clearly exist as hubs. An additional forum (the Sep 3 environmental event) is comparable in content but advocacy-organized, not neutral, so it's flagged rather than counted as Tier 1.
