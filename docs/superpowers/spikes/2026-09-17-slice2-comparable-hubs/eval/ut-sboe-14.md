# Utah State Board of Education, District 14 — 2026 (slug: ut-sboe-14)

Candidates (as given): Nichole Isom, Courtnee Justice, Linda Hanks, Will Pierce.
District 14 covers Beaver, Carbon, Emery, Grand, Juab, Millard, Sanpete, Sevier Counties + part of Iron and Utah Counties (rural central/southern Utah).

**Ground-truth correction found during research (verified on Ballotpedia):** only Hanks and Isom actually advanced past the April 25, 2026 Republican ranked-choice convention (Hanks 51.8%, Isom 48.2% in round 2); Justice and Pierce were eliminated in round 1 and never reached the primary ballot. Hanks then beat Isom in the June 23, 2026 primary (69.4%–30.6%). The Democratic convention winner (Danielle Stratton) subsequently withdrew, leaving Hanks unopposed for the Nov 3, 2026 general as of this check. This matters for interpreting "richness" below — most of the real contest activity is Hanks-vs-Isom, not a 4-way race.

## Richness summary

This race is thin, as expected for a rural down-ballot state-board seat. The single strongest source is **Ballotpedia's Candidate Connection survey**, and even there only 1 of 4 candidates (Isom) actually filled it in — Hanks, Justice, and Pierce all have live, prompting pages that remain blank. No debate, forum, or interview (spoken, tiers 1–2) was found anywhere for this specific 2026 race, despite a real organizing body (Utah Education Debate Coalition) existing and having run D14 debates in prior cycles — it simply hasn't been refreshed for 2026, plausibly because the general is now uncontested (Hanks only). A neutral local newspaper (Princeton Herald analog for Utah would be the rural county papers) never published a Q&A. The two written-questionnaire hubs that do exist (iVoterGuide, Utah Parents United) are both advocacy-flavored and both came back empty/stale for this race. Absent tiers: 1 (debate/forum) — none for 2026; 2 (interview) — none found at all.
