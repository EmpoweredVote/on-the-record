## Organizing bodies (hubs) for this jurisdiction
- **Citizens Clean Elections Commission** (azcleanelections.gov) — state debate commission AND publisher of the written Voter Education Guide; the central hub for both tier-1 and tier-3 material statewide.
- **Arizona Media Association + Arizona Local News Foundation** (azmedia.org) — co-produces/distributes the CCEC debate series.
- **League of Women Voters of Arizona** / VOTE411.org — nonpartisan written voter guide, LWV-run.
- **KJZZ** (Phoenix NPR affiliate) and **AZPM** (Tucson NPR/PBS) — voter-guide hubs, host occasional one-on-one interviews, rebroadcast CCEC debates.
- **Arizona PBS / Cronkite News** (ASU) — "Arizona Horizon" / "AZ Votes" debate and interview franchise.
- **KTAR** (commercial news radio) — debate co-broadcaster and forum moderator (e.g., Arizona Police Association forum).
